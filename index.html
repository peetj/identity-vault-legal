Identity Vault — Privacy Policy

Developer: Nexgen STEM School · Effective 16 September 2026

The short version: Identity Vault is a fully offline app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. We do not collect, use, sell or share any personal data. Everything you store stays encrypted on your own device.

1. Who we are

Identity Vault is published by Nexgen STEM School (Nexgen Codecamp Pty Ltd), Australia. Contact: apps@nexgenstemschool.com.au.

2. What we collect

Nothing. There is no account or sign-up, no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, no trackers, and no third-party data-sharing SDKs. Because nothing is collected, there is no personal data held by us to disclose, sell, or hand over on request.

3. No internet access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission. It cannot upload, sync or transmit your information. It works fully in aeroplane mode.

4. Where your data lives

  • Your entries, photos and documents are stored in an encrypted database and encrypted files inside the app's private storage on your device. Other apps cannot read that storage.
  • Encryption keys are derived from your master password (Argon2id) and protected with the device's hardware-backed keystore. An optional Sensitive PIN additionally encrypts the fields and files you mark as sensitive.
  • We never see your master password, PIN, or any decrypted content, and we hold no copy of your data.

5. Permissions and why we ask

PermissionWhy
USE_BIOMETRIC (optional) Lets you unlock with your fingerprint or face instead of typing the password. Biometric matching is performed by Android; biometric data never leaves your device and is never available to the app.

When you attach a photo or document, the app uses Android's system file picker, so you choose a specific file — the selected file is then copied into your encrypted vault. The app does not request broad storage access.

6. Backups and exports

The app does not upload backups anywhere. A backup only exists if you create one: the export produces an encrypted .ivault file, and you choose where it goes. Once you save or share that file (for example, to your own cloud drive), it is outside the app — and outside our control — so protect it as you would any sensitive document.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete individual items, photos or documents in the app.
  • Use Settings to clear vault contents; the app's anti-theft settings (auto-wipe after failed attempts, auto-destroy on inactivity) can erase everything automatically.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data from the device. Device backups for this app are disabled, so your vault is not copied into cloud backups.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: apps@nexgenstemschool.com.au.
